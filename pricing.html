Questions

Pricing questions

How much does a website cost?

Starter is $5 for 1–3 pages, Business is $10 for 4–6 pages, and Pro is $15 for 7–10 pages. It’s a one-time price, not a monthly bill, and you pay only after your site is done.

Why are your prices so low?

Our goal is to help local businesses save time and make more money, not to charge a ridiculous amount. We’d rather walk away knowing we helped.

When do I pay?

After your site is built and you’ve had a chance to look it over. No deposit, nothing upfront.

Can customers book appointments on my site?

Yes. Online booking is included in every plan, so customers can book themselves while you work.

Do I need a meeting first?

No. Pick a plan and answer a few quick questions about your colors, logo, and style. If we need anything else, we’ll reach out.

What if I need more than 10 pages?

Tell us what you need in your answers and we’ll give you a clear quote before any work starts.
