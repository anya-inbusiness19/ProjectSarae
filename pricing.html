Questions

Website design pricing FAQ

How much does a small business website cost?

Project Sarae plans start at $50 a month, with hosting, updates, and support included. Bigger custom projects and full website redesigns get a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you design websites for hair salons and med spas?

Yes. Salon and med spa website design is our specialty, with service menus, stylist or provider pages, online booking, and appointment reminders built in.

Can my clients book appointments online?

Yes. Online booking, reminders, and follow-ups are set up starting with the Starter plan, so clients can book while you work and fewer people no-show.

Do you only work with businesses in Huntsville?

We're based in Huntsville, AL, and work with local businesses here and anywhere in the US. Everything is planned over a free Zoom call.

Do I need Wix or Squarespace?

No. We build and host your custom website for you, so there's no website builder to learn and nothing technical for you to manage.
