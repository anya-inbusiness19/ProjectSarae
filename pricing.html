Questions

Pricing questions

How much does a website cost?

Starter is $5 a month for 1–3 pages, Business is $10 a month for 4–6 pages, and Pro is $15 a month for 7–10 pages.

Why are your prices so low?

Our goal is to help local businesses save time and make more money, not to charge a ridiculous amount. We’d rather walk away knowing we helped.

When do I pay?

Your first monthly payment is due after your site is built and you’ve had a chance to look it over. No deposit, nothing upfront.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There’s no contract. Your plan stays active through the month you paid for.

Can customers book appointments on my site?

Yes. Online booking is included in every plan, even Starter, so customers can book themselves while you work.

Do I need a meeting first?

No. Pick a plan and answer a few quick questions about your colors, logo, and style. If we need anything else, we’ll reach out.

What if I need more than 10 pages?

Tell us what you need in your answers and we’ll give you a clear quote before any work starts.
