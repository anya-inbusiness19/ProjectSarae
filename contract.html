1. Parties

This Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is between A’Nya Hill, doing business as Project Sarae (“Project Sarae,” “we,” or “us”) and the client who signs up for a plan (“Client” or “you”). The Agreement starts on the day you sign up (the “Start Date”).

2. Services

We will design, build, launch, host, and maintain a website for your business according to the plan you choose (Starter, Business, or Pro) and any add-ons you select. What each plan includes is listed on the Pricing page as of your Start Date. Services include:

  • Building your site from the answers you give when you sign up. If we need more information, we’ll reach out.
  • Up to two rounds of revisions before your site launches.
  • Launch and hosting while your plan is active.
  • Small updates while your plan is active, such as new hours, prices, photos, or wording.

New pages beyond your plan’s page limit, new features, and redesigns are not small updates. We will quote them in writing before starting any work.

  • Nothing due upfront: There is no setup fee and no deposit. Your first payment is due when your site is ready and you’ve had a chance to review it.
  • If you don’t continue: If you choose not to start your plan once your site is ready, you owe nothing and your site is not launched.

  • Processing fees: Card processing fees charged by Stripe or Square on your own customers’ purchases go to those companies. We do not add hidden fees.
  • Price changes: We will give you at least 30 days’ written notice before changing your price. You may cancel before the change takes effect.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Provide your logo, photos, business hours, services, prices, and other content within a reasonable time. Delays in providing content may delay your site.
  • Confirm that you own or have permission to use everything you send us, including photos, text, and logos.
  • Make sure the information on your site, such as prices, hours, and policies, is accurate. Review your site before it launches.
  • Keep any account logins you hold (for example Stripe or your domain registrar) secure.

5. Term and cancellation

  • Term: There is no minimum term. The Agreement continues month to month until canceled.
  • Canceling: You may cancel your plan or any add-on anytime by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you’ve paid for, and your site stays live until then.
  • Ending by us: We may end this Agreement with 30 days’ written notice. If we do, we will refund any prepaid fees for time after the end date and help transfer your site files and domain.

6. Refunds

  • Payments are not refunded when you cancel, including plan and add-on payments.
  • Exception: Accidental or mistaken charges (for example, a duplicate payment or a charge made in error) are refunded in full. Contact us within 30 days of the charge.

See the Terms & Refund Policy for more detail.

7. Late or missed payments

If a payment fails, we will send reminders. After a 14-day grace period, we may pause your website until the balance is paid. Your content is kept while the site is paused. If a balance remains unpaid for 60 days, we may end this Agreement.

8. Ownership

  • Your content: You keep ownership of all content you provide (text, photos, logos).
  • Your domain: Any custom domain is registered in your name, and you keep it if you leave.
  • Your website files: After 3 paid months, if your account is paid in full, we will give you a copy of your site’s files when you cancel, and you may use them however you like.
  • Our tools: We keep ownership of our general templates, code, and design methods. You receive a permanent license to use them as part of your website.
  • Portfolio: We may show your website in our portfolio unless you ask us not to in writing.

9. Third-party services

Your site may use outside services such as Stripe, Square, GitHub, Supabase, Brevo, or booking tools. Those services have their own terms and fees. We are not responsible for their outages, policy changes, or decisions, but we will work with you to fix any problems they cause on your site.

10. No guarantees

We set up your site to help customers find and book you, but we cannot guarantee search rankings, website traffic, sales, or uninterrupted uptime.

11. Limit of liability

To the extent allowed by law, our total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or lost-profit damages.

12. Confidentiality

We will keep private any non-public business information you share with us and use it only to provide the services.

13. General

  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama.
  • We will try to resolve any disagreement by talking first, in good faith.
  • Changes to this Agreement must be in writing (email is fine) and agreed to by both of us.
  • If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest still applies.
  • Checking the agreement box when you sign up is your electronic signature and has the same effect as a handwritten signature.
