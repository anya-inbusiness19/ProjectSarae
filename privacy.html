Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language template, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it for your business.

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

  • Anonymous visit data: which page you viewed, which website sent you, your device type (phone, tablet, or computer), and buttons you clicked. A random ID, kept only for your browser session, groups visits together. We don’t collect your name, IP address, or exact location for analytics.
  • Sign-up details: when you pick a plan, we collect your name, business name and type, email, phone number, how and when you’d like to be contacted, your style choices (colors, font, look), the features you want, any notes, and your logo if you upload one. You also create a password, which is stored securely and is never visible to us.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email, phone, business name, and message. These are delivered to our email through FormSubmit.
  • Reviews: the name, business name, rating, and review you submit.
  • Payment details: Stripe handles your payment information. We see that a payment was made, but never your full card number.
  • Emails: anything you send us directly.

How we use it

To build and run your website, respond to you, send updates about your order, bill for services, and understand which parts of this site are helpful. If you choose an email add-on, we use the details needed to send those emails for your business. We never sell your information.

Services that help run this site

GitHub Pages (hosting), Supabase (database and accounts), Stripe (payments), FormSubmit (contact form delivery), Brevo (emails), and Google Fonts (text styling). Each of these has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a random visit ID in your browser that is deleted when you close the tab. While you fill out the sign-up questions, your answers are saved in your browser so you don’t lose them, and they’re cleared once you finish. Signing in to your account keeps you logged in on that browser until you sign out.

How long we keep it

Visit data: up to 2 years. Account, order, and billing records: as long as needed for business and tax records. Contact messages: as long as needed to help you. Reviews: until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information by emailing . We’ll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
